Privacy Policy

Bloqivra: Block Puzzle · iOS and Android apps

Effective date: October 18, 2026 (see section 12 for the revision history)

한국어 · Support

Summary

  • Bloqivra is a puzzle game with no sign-up or login. The game can be played without an internet connection.
  • The developer does not collect personal data such as your name or email address and runs no servers or analytics tools of its own.
  • Game progress (best score, game in progress, language choice and similar) is stored only on your device.
  • Versions that include ads have rewarded ads that you watch only when you choose to. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, and Google processes some device information to show them (section 2).
  • Ads are requested as non-personalized ads only. On iPhone, after the ad consent check, the app asks once for App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission. Whatever you choose, the game and the ad continue work the same (section 3).
  • Where consent is required, the app asks for it first, and it does not request ads unless ad requests are allowed (section 3).

1. Scope

This policy applies to the iOS and Android versions of the app "Bloqivra: Block Puzzle" (the "app"), provided on the App Store and Google Play by SANG HUN SONG (the "developer").

  • iOS app version 1.0 has no ads; the ad-related parts of sections 2 and 3 do not apply to it.
  • Rewarded ads apply from the later versions that include the ad feature. Each store's app page shows whether the app contains ads.

The web hosting service that publishes this page may process access logs under its own policy when you visit the page. This is separate from how the app handles information.

2. Rewarded ads and how Google processes information

When are ads shown? Only when the game is over and you tap "Watch an ad to continue" yourself. There are no banner ads and no ads that appear automatically. If you watch the ad to the end, you get 3 new blocks on the same board with the same score, once per game.

Ad provider: Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK), an advertising service of Google LLC. The app does not connect any other ad network.

Information Google processes: While loading and showing an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process the following information, as described by Google.

  • No personalized ads. The app requests every ad as a non-personalized ad. Even for non-personalized ads, Google may process the information above for frequency capping, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention.
  • iPhone and tracking: In the privacy declaration it provides to Apple, the Google Mobile Ads SDK states that it uses device identifiers for "tracking" (combining them with data from other companies' apps or websites to deliver and measure ads).   • Without an internet connection no ads are loaded, and the game still works normally.

3. Ad consent and your choices

  • Consent check: The app uses Google's consent management tool (User Messaging Platform, UMP) to check whether consent is required where you are. In regions that require consent, such as the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK and Switzerland, a consent form is shown when the app starts.
  • When ads are requested: The app requests ads only when UMP allows ad requests. If you have not made a choice yet, if your consent status cannot be determined, or if ad requests are not allowed, the app does not request ads. In that case only "Watch an ad to continue" is unavailable; everything else in the game works as usual.
  • If you do not consent: Even if you decline on the consent form, once your choice is recorded Google may, under its own policies, serve ads with limited use of personal data or serve no ads. In every case the app requests non-personalized ads only.
  • Changing or withdrawing consent: In regions that require consent, tap "Ad privacy settings" on the start screen to change or withdraw your choice at any time. After a change, an ad that was already loaded is not used, and the app checks your consent status again right before showing an ad.
  • You never have to watch ads: Ads appear only when you tap the button, so you can keep playing without watching any ad.
  • iPhone tracking permission (ATT): On iPhone, only after the ad consent check has finished and ad requests are allowed, the app asks once whether to "Allow app to track", if you have not chosen yet. If you do not allow it, the game and the ad continue work the same. You can change your choice at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID: You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings under Privacy (or Google) → Ads. Menu names vary by device.

4. Information we do not collect

The developer does not collect the following information, and the app does not ask for it:

  • Contact information such as your name, email address or phone number (the app has no sign-up or login)
  • Precise location such as GPS, contacts, photos, camera or microphone
  • Payment information (there are no in-app purchases)

The developer has not added any servers, analytics tools or crash-reporting tools to the app. Information Google processes for ads is described in section 2.

6. Use of device features

  • Language settings: On first launch the app reads your device's language settings to show Korean or English. This is used only on the device and is not stored or transmitted. If you change the language in the app, only that choice is stored, as listed above.
  • Vibration (haptics): The app uses haptic feedback when you place blocks or clear lines. No information is collected in the process.
  • Internet: Used only for ads and the consent check. Playing the game does not need the internet.

7. Information handled by the app stores

The app is downloaded and updated through Apple's App Store or Google Play, and each company handles the related information under its own policy. If you have agreed in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, the store operator may provide the developer with crash information or usage statistics in a form that does not identify you. For details, see the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Sharing and sale

The developer does not sell your personal data, and because the developer collects no personal data directly, there is none to share with third parties. In versions that include ads, the information in section 2 is sent to Google through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, and Google processes it under its own privacy policy.

9. Retention and deletion

  • Information stored on your device is kept while the app is installed.
  • When you choose "New Game" or "Restart", or when a game ends, the previous game-in-progress data is replaced or deleted.
  • Deleting the app also deletes the app's information and the ad consent status stored on your device. If you use device backups (such as iCloud Backup or Google backup), information included in a backup may remain according to your backup settings.
  • How long Google keeps ad-related information is governed by Google's policies. On Android you can reset or delete the advertising ID.

10. Children's privacy

The app is not primarily directed at children. The developer does not directly collect personal data from any user, including children, and ads are limited to content rated for general audiences (General).

11. Security

Information the app stores is kept in a storage area that iOS and Android keep separate for each app, and it is not shared with other apps or with the developer's servers. Google states that the information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

12. Changes to this policy

If a feature that changes how information is handled is added or changed, we will revise this policy and post the effective date and the changes on this page before releasing that update.
