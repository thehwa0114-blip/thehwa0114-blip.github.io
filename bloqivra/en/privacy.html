Privacy Policy

Bloqivra: Block Puzzle · iOS app version 1.0

Effective date: October 16, 2026

한국어 · Support

Summary

  • Bloqivra is an offline puzzle game. There is no sign-up or login.
  • The app does not collect personal data and does not send any information to the developer's or any third party's servers.
  • The information the game needs (best score, game in progress, language choice) is stored only on your iPhone.
  • This version contains no ads, analytics, tracking or in-app purchases.

1. Scope

This policy applies to version 1.0 of the iOS app "Bloqivra: Block Puzzle" (the "app"), provided on the App Store by SANG HUN SONG (the "developer").

The web hosting service that publishes this page may process access logs under its own policy when you visit the page. This is separate from how the app handles information.

2. Information we do not collect

The app does not collect or transmit:

  • Contact information such as your name, email address or phone number (the app has no sign-up or login)
  • Device identifiers such as the advertising identifier (IDFA)
  • Location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data
  • Usage history, analytics data or crash reports (the app includes no analytics or crash-reporting tools)
  • Payment information (there are no in-app purchases)

The app has no feature that sends data over the internet, and every feature works without an internet connection.

3. Information stored only on your device

So that you can pick up where you left off, the app stores the following in its own storage area on your iPhone. The app does not send this information to the developer or any third party, and the developer cannot see it. (For iPhone backups, see section 7.)

ItemContentsPurpose
Best scoreYour highest score so farShowing your best score
Game in progressBoard, remaining blocks, current score, combo, number of moves, lines clearedContinuing the game when you reopen the app
Language choiceThe display language you picked (Korean or English)Showing the same language next time
Internal settingA marker showing that a one-time storage migration has finishedInternal app operation

4. Use of device features

  • Language settings: On first launch the app reads your device's language settings to show Korean or English. This is used only on the device and is not stored or transmitted. If you change the language in the app, only that choice is stored, as listed above.
  • Vibration (haptics): The app uses haptic feedback when you place blocks or clear lines. No information is collected in the process.

5. Information handled by Apple

The app is downloaded and updated through Apple's App Store, and Apple handles the related information under its own policy. If you have agreed in your iPhone settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide the developer with crash information or usage statistics in a form that does not identify you. For details, see the Apple Privacy Policy.

6. Sharing and sale

Because the app does not collect personal data, there is no information to share with or sell to third parties.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Information stored on your device is kept while the app is installed.
  • When you choose "New Game" or "Restart", or when a game ends, the previous game-in-progress data is replaced or deleted.
  • Deleting the app also deletes the app's information stored on your device. If you use iPhone backups (such as iCloud Backup), information included in a backup may remain according to your backup settings.

8. Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal data from any user, including children.

9. Security

Information the app stores is kept in a storage area that iOS keeps separate for each app, and it is not shared with other apps or with the developer's servers.

10. Changes to this policy

If a new feature (for example, ads) changes how the app handles information, we will revise this policy and post the effective date and the changes on this page before releasing the update that includes that feature.
