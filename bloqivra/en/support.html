About the app

Bloqivra is a block puzzle game: place blocks on a 10×10 board to fill rows or columns and clear them. You can play right away without signing up, and it works without an internet connection.

  • Devices: iPhone (iOS 15.0 or later), Android 7.0 or later (once the Android app is released)
  • Languages: 한국어 (Korean), English
  • There are no in-app purchases.
  • Ads: iOS version 1.0 has no ads. The Android app includes rewarded ads, which you watch only when you choose to, from its first version (1.0). Each store's app page shows whether the app contains ads.

How to play

  • Drag one of the three blocks at the bottom onto the board.
  • When a row or column is full, it clears and you earn points.
  • Clear lines again within a few moves to keep a combo going and boost your points.
  • The game ends when none of the remaining blocks fit on the board.

FAQ

Is my game in progress saved?
Yes. It is saved automatically every time you place a block. When you reopen the app, tap "Continue" on the start screen to keep playing.

Where is my best score stored?
Your best score and game in progress are stored only on the device you are using. There are no accounts, so they cannot be moved to another device or restored from a server.

What happens to my records if I delete the app?
Deleting the app also deletes the best score and game in progress stored on your device.

How do I change the language?
Tap the language button ("EN" or "한") on the start screen or at the top of the game screen to switch between Korean and English. Your choice is kept the next time you open the app.

How do I start over?
Tap the restart button at the top of the game screen and confirm to start a new game. Your best score is kept.

When are ads shown? (Android app and other versions with ads)
Only when the game is over and you tap "Watch an ad to continue" yourself. If you watch the ad to the end, you get 3 new blocks on the same board with the same score, once per game. You can always keep playing with "Play Again" without watching an ad.

I can't tap "Watch an ad to continue".
You may be offline, no ad may be available right now, or your ad privacy settings may not allow ad requests. The game still works normally. If you close the ad early, the continue is not applied and you can try again.

Where do I change my ad privacy settings?
In regions that require consent (European Economic Area, UK, Switzerland and others), tap "Ad privacy settings" on the start screen at any time. In US states with privacy laws, such as California, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information in the same "Ad privacy settings". On Android you can also reset or delete the advertising ID in the Ads menu of your device settings. On iPhone you can change whether the app may track you at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you do not allow it, the game and the "continue with an ad" option work the same.
