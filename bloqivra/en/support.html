About the app

Bloqivra is a block puzzle game: place blocks on a 10×10 board to fill rows or columns and clear them. You can play right away without signing up, and it works without an internet connection.

  • Devices: iPhone (iOS 15.0 or later)
  • Languages: 한국어 (Korean), English
  • This version has no ads and no in-app purchases.

How to play

  • Drag one of the three blocks at the bottom onto the board.
  • When a row or column is full, it clears and you earn points.
  • Clear lines again within a few moves to keep a combo going and boost your points.
  • The game ends when none of the remaining blocks fit on the board.

FAQ

Is my game in progress saved?
Yes. It is saved automatically every time you place a block. When you reopen the app, tap "Continue" on the start screen to keep playing.

Where is my best score stored?
Your best score and game in progress are stored only on the iPhone you are using. There are no accounts, so they cannot be moved to another device or restored from a server.

What happens to my records if I delete the app?
Deleting the app also deletes the best score and game in progress stored on your device.

How do I change the language?
Tap the language button ("EN" or "한") on the start screen or at the top of the game screen to switch between Korean and English. Your choice is kept the next time you open the app.

How do I start over?
Tap the restart button at the top of the game screen and confirm to start a new game. Your best score is kept.
